## Deviations / judgment calls
1. **Dead-hang band anchor = 15 s** (the spec's "<15–20 s" window read as a fuzzy transition; 15 chosen as the inclusive lower edge, documented in-code).
2. **Pull-up band precedence is evidence-ranked**: negatives > rows≥8 > rows≥3 > hang≥15 > foundation-missing; rows 1–2 fall through to the hang logic (a couple of rows is not a band).
3. **A MINOR muscle reads HIGH honestly** (grip at <15 s hang — the first practical limiter of a cold rep), where R31-a minors capped at moderate.
4. **Rows/negatives have no metric type** (only 3 new types were sanctioned) → they are RECORDS-sourced with local-only steppers whose honest persistence path is "log a pull session".
5. **Run equipment axis is none/track, NOT the gym tiers** — a separate RunEquipment type + runEquipmentAllows + ROADS/TRACK tags; track access is always a local-only answer (no server surface implies a track).
6. **5K minutes entry is seconds-with-live-preview** rather than a minute+second pair — one stepper, "≈ 25:00 FOR 5 K · 5:00 /KM" echo line, and the 1K stepper previews its own equivalent.
7. **Riegel 5^1.06 exponent, CONSENSUS-framed** ("standard pace-equivalency, an equivalent, not a guarantee") — never cited to an author.
8. **Jump catalog is 18 picks, not the worklog's "16"** (R31-a shipped 18 across 6 groups; pinned in scratch QA as the real count).
9. **METRIC bounds kept spec-verbatim** (best5kSec floor 600 is sub-world-record — a typo fence in the house sanity-fence idiom, like verticalJumpCm's 120 max; documented in the route comment).
10. **Resting-HR stepper** appears on the 5K questionnaire only when the vitals reading is missing (KAEL has 58 → hidden in QA); a save would legitimately grant the vitals check-in XP like the Fastrack card.
