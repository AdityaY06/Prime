- Evidence always renders all 6 signal rows with honest "NOT LOGGED" (stable card, shows what to log) rather than only firing signals; week shape rides the TRAIN-default rationale instead of a 7th row (spec caps evidence at ~6).
- Coaches on integer values render without trailing .0 (health.ts signed1/fmt1 idiom), e.g. "−12 · WATCH" — house formatting, spec examples show fractional values.
